# The pile

A palette, not a recipe. Expect two or three ingredients to lead and the rest to flavor now and then.

Rules for drawing from it are in `playbook.md` under "Rules for sparks". Rejected sparks go to `tank.md`.

| Ingredient | What to borrow |
| --- | --- |
| Parachute opening | Mid-action start, instant stakes, built-in clock |
| Life flashing before your eyes | Each choice a memory during the fall; licenses big jumps |
| BitLife / Game of Life | Deadpan result lines, coin flips for luck, ending report cards, time jumps |
| Money | Coins that open choices; tracked on a wallet bookmark or remembered by the path |
| 70s/80s movie tropes | Red wire or blue wire, the gang on bikes, grown-ups don't believe you, montage, freeze-frame endings. Shapes, not name-drops |
| Jodorowsky | Dream logic, tarot symbolism (The Fool steps off a cliff), recurring symbolic objects. A light sprinkle |
| Roald Dahl | Ridiculous grown-ups, satisfying comeuppance, playful words, dark but safe, a narrator who talks straight to the reader |
| Robert Crumb | Scratchy drawing style, poking fun at money. Attitude only |
| Richard Scarry | Calm captions over chaos, everyone has a job, background gags, recurring clumsy character |
| Xavier: Renegade Angel | The confidently wrong guru, wordplay chains, laughing at the mystical layer. A pinch |
